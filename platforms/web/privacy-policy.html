Privacy Policy for ReferenceFrame

Last Updated: January 2026

Summary: ReferenceFrame works entirely offline and collects zero data. Your information stays on your device.

Overview

ReferenceFrame is a picture framing calculator app. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

Data Collection

We do not collect any data.

ReferenceFrame:

  • Does not collect personal information
  • Does not collect usage analytics
  • Does not track your location
  • Does not use cookies or similar technologies
  • Does not share any data with third parties
  • Does not require an account or registration
  • Does not connect to external servers

Local Storage

The app stores your preferences and saved configurations locally on your device only. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not accessible to us or any third party
  • Can be deleted by uninstalling the app

Photo Library Access

The app requests photo library access solely to save exported PDF diagrams to your device. We do not read, upload, or access any existing photos.

Children's Privacy

ReferenceFrame does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the new version here with an updated date.
