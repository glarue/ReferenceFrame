Cut Pieces Visualization Example

This demonstrates the cut pieces view for a typical 8×12" artwork frame with 2" mat and 0.75" molding. The trapezoids show the actual cut geometry with 45° mitered ends.
Frame Specifications:
• Artwork: 8" × 12"
• Mat: 2" all sides
• Frame material width: 0.75"
• Rabbet: 0.375"
• Inside (cut) dimensions: 11.75" × 15.75"
• Outside dimensions: 13.25" × 17.25"
• Scale: 40 px/inch (matches plan view)
Note: The faint red dashed lines show the 45° miter cuts (for visualization only, not shown in final design). The trapezoid shapes represent the actual cut pieces as viewed from above.
