Privacy Policy for ReferenceFrame

Last Updated: September 2026

Summary: ReferenceFrame collects zero data. Your designs and settings stay on your device. The iOS app works entirely offline; the web version loads a few standard libraries from public servers (details below).

Overview

ReferenceFrame is a picture framing calculator available as an iOS app and as a web app. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information on both.

Data Collection

We do not collect any data.

ReferenceFrame:

  • Does not collect personal information
  • Does not collect usage analytics
  • Does not track your location
  • Does not use cookies or tracking technologies
  • Does not share any data with third parties
  • Does not require an account or registration
  • Does not send your designs or settings anywhere

Network Connections

iOS app: does not connect to any external servers.

Web app: is hosted on GitHub Pages, and loads fonts and code libraries (for icons and PDF export) from Google Fonts, cdnjs (Cloudflare), and unpkg. As with any website, these providers receive standard request information such as your IP address and browser type when your browser downloads these files; their own privacy policies apply. None of your designs, measurements, or settings are included in these requests.

If you use the web app's "Share" feature, the design is encoded in the link itself; it is only shared with whomever you choose to send the link to.

Local Storage

ReferenceFrame stores your preferences, saved configurations, and design history locally on your device only (in the app's storage on iOS, or your browser's local storage for the web app). This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not accessible to us or any third party
  • Can be deleted by uninstalling the app, or by clearing this site's data in your browser

Permissions

ReferenceFrame does not require any device permissions. PDF export uses the system print dialog, and text sharing uses the system share sheet — neither accesses your photos, camera, or other personal data.

Children's Privacy

ReferenceFrame does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the new version here with an updated date.
